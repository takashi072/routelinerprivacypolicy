# Privacy Policy

[Route Liner] (hereinafter referred to as "the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
To provide and improve our services, the App collects and processes the following information:

* **Location Data (GPS and network-based location)**
  * **Purpose of Use**: The App continuously collects and processes your location data, even when the App is closed or not in use (in the background).
  * **Data Handling**: Your location data is processed strictly within your device to provide these features. We do not transmit, store, or share your location data to external servers or any third parties.
  * **Battery Consumption**: Please note that continuous use of location services running in the background can significantly decrease battery life.

## 2. Personal Information Management
The App does not collect, record, or store any other personally identifiable information (PII), such as your name, email address, or phone number. We never sell or disclose your location data to third parties.

## 3. How to Opt-Out of Location Tracking
You can enable or disable location tracking at any time by adjusting your device settings ("Settings" > "Privacy & Security" > "Location Services" > select "[Your App Name]"). If you disable location permissions, some features of the App may not function properly.
